Three tools for regular communication with families about progress,
achievements, and behavior.

1. **Monthly class newsletter** sent through the school's messaging platform and
   Google Classroom guardian summaries. It covers current projects, upcoming
   deadlines, ways to support coding at home, and links to student work. I will
   write it in plain English so families can use translation tools, and I will
   ask the school for translated versions when available.
2. **Personal positive messages.** Every student's family receives at least one
   specific good-news message each term through the channel and language the
   family prefers, as noted in school records and the student background survey.
   Each message invites families to reply, keeping communication two-way before
   any problem arises (U.S. Department of Education, 2023).
3. **Student-led project showcases and conferences.** Twice a year, students
   present their code and learning goals to their families. Sessions are in
   person and also livestreamed or recorded for families who are traveling or
   living in other time zones. If family communication is not permitted in a
   placement, I would send the same progress summaries to the school
   administrator and my mentor teacher.
