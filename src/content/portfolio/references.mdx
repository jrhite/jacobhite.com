Every source cited anywhere in this portfolio, gathered into one list. Each page
keeps its own reference list — that is where APA puts it, and it is what prints
with the page — but the entries themselves are written once, in a single file,
so a correction lands everywhere at once. This page is that file, read out.

The line under each entry says which pages cite it.

> **On the letters.** APA adds an *a* / *b* / *c* to works by the same author in
> the same year, so a reader can tell which one a citation points at. The letter
> belongs to the list, not to the work: a source is *n.d.* on a page where it
> stands alone and *n.d.-b* here, where two others share its author and year.
> Both are correct, and each page's own list is the one its in-text citations
> match.
