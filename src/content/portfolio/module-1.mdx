### Unit 1, Activity 1: Orientation to Moreland University
The ED Puzzle was a great introduction to the Moreland teaching platform, including
its goals and pointers to some of the most important elements of the TEACH-NOW
website including:

- where the Course Catalog and Candidate Handbook can be found (Activity Resources and Library)
- the goal and purposes of Moreland University
- the attendance policy of the program, and how to handle a missed class
- the grading policy
- how to access the cohort's Google Drive (from the Google Drive icon on the Learn page)
- the Home page and the course outline, and the importance of Modules 7 and 8, the student teaching portion of the program

The InTASC Standards outline what teachers should know and be able to do to ensure
that every K–12 student reaches the goal of being ready to enter college or the
workforce in today's world. They get their own page here — see
[InTASC Standards](/portfolio/intasc-standards).

This site. The activity was to create an e-portfolio for the program — a home

### Unit 1, Activity 3: Introduction to literacy
Each module the cohort adds to a shared Literacy Guide, organized by module with
terms listed alphabetically. Every member defines the terms for the module
currently in session, so Module 1's section is the program-orientation vocabulary.

It lives on the [Literacy](/portfolio/literacy) page.
