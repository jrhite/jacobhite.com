### A plan for the classroom I will walk into

This is the plan I will bring to my clinical teaching practice: a secondary
computer science classroom in an international school, with students from all
over the world. It covers how I will build relationships, set norms, run the
room, and support students when something gets in the way, both in person and
online.

I built it one step at a time through Module 4 of my TEACH-NOW program, Managing
the Learning Environment. Each step also appears in the activity that asked for
it.
